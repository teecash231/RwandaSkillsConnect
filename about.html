About Rwanda SkillsConnect

Empowering Rwanda's digital economy by connecting talented freelancers with businesses that need their skills.

Our Mission

To create opportunities for skilled professionals across Rwanda while helping businesses access the talent they need to grow.

Building Rwanda's Digital Future

Rwanda SkillsConnect is more than just a freelancing platform. We're building a community that supports the growth of Rwanda's digital economy by:

  • Connecting local talent with global opportunities
  • Supporting skill development and capacity building
  • Fostering innovation and entrepreneurship
Vision 2030

Supporting Rwanda's vision to become a knowledge-based economy through digital transformation and skills development.

Our Values
